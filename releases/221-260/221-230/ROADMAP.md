# Roadmap

| Release | Capability | Outcome |
|---:|---|---|
| 221 | Enterprise Service Topology | Map services, dependencies, owners and critical paths. |
| 222 | Configuration and Asset Intelligence | Govern asset and configuration records with provenance and reconciliation. |
| 223 | Incident Intelligence and Triage | Correlate signals, severity, ownership and evidence. |
| 224 | Problem and Root-Cause Management | Track recurring failures, hypotheses, causes and corrective actions. |
| 225 | Change Risk Intelligence | Assess blast radius, tests, approvals and rollback. |
| 226 | Runbook and Remediation Orchestration | Execute bounded, approved and reversible runbooks. |
| 227 | Service-Level and Error-Budget Operations | Operate SLOs, error budgets and release consequences. |
| 228 | Operations Capacity and Demand Forecasting | Forecast demand, saturation and dependency constraints. |
| 229 | Operational Cost and Efficiency Management | Attribute cost and enforce free-first budget controls. |
| 230 | Enterprise Operations Assurance Gate | Gate operations on evidence and recovery readiness. |
