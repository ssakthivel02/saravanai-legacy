# Releases 561–570 roadmap

    | Release | Capability | Intended outcome |
    |---:|---|---|
    | 561 | Emergency Information Verification | Verify emergency facts, sources, timestamps, uncertainty, corrections and publication authority. |
| 562 | Crisis Alerting and Notification | Deliver accessible, multilingual, location-appropriate alerts with approval and correction controls. |
| 563 | Disaster Response Resource Coordination | Coordinate shelters, supplies, transport, availability, owners, priorities and audit evidence. |
| 564 | Humanitarian Data Protection | Protect vulnerable-person data through minimisation, purpose, access, retention and safe sharing. |
| 565 | Missing Person and Family Reunification Safety | Handle sensitive reports, consent, identity, verification, exposure limits and escalation. |
| 566 | Public Health Information Boundary | Publish non-clinical public information with qualified review, sources, uncertainty and referral. |
| 567 | Essential Service Continuity | Coordinate power, water, communications, transport and digital-service continuity dependencies. |
| 568 | Volunteer and Community Coordination | Register volunteers, roles, safeguarding, competence, scheduling, support and deactivation. |
| 569 | Public Event and Crowd Safety Information | Publish verified capacity, access, travel, emergency and accessibility information. |
| 570 | Public Safety and Humanitarian Assurance Gate | Gate emergency capabilities on verification, privacy, accessibility, safeguarding and continuity. |
