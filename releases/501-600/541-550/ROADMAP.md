# Releases 541–550 roadmap

    | Release | Capability | Intended outcome |
    |---:|---|---|
    | 541 | Identity Proofing and Assurance | Govern identity evidence, assurance levels, verification, exceptions, fraud and review. |
| 542 | Credential Issuance and Lifecycle | Issue, bind, rotate, suspend, revoke and audit credentials with owner accountability. |
| 543 | Passwordless and Passkey Readiness | Assess device support, recovery, attestation, phishing resistance, accessibility and fallback. |
| 544 | Verifiable Credential Readiness | Assess issuer, holder, verifier, schema, revocation, privacy and interoperability requirements. |
| 545 | Delegated Guardian and Representative Access | Govern carers, guardians, representatives and delegates through scope, evidence and expiry. |
| 546 | Privileged Identity Governance v2 | Control privileged eligibility, activation, approvals, session evidence, review and emergency access. |
| 547 | Identity Recovery Fraud and Redress | Handle account recovery, fraud claims, identity disputes, evidence, correction and redress. |
| 548 | Federation and Trust Broker Operations | Manage trust relationships, metadata, keys, claims, mappings, monitoring and termination. |
| 549 | Identity Privacy and Selective Disclosure | Minimise identity attributes and support purpose-bound disclosure, consent and revocation. |
| 550 | Identity Trust Assurance Gate | Gate identity services on proofing, credential, recovery, delegation, privilege, privacy and redress. |
