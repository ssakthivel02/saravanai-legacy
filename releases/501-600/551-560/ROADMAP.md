# Releases 551–560 roadmap

    | Release | Capability | Intended outcome |
    |---:|---|---|
    | 551 | Unified Work Queue and Personal Productivity | Coordinate assigned work, priorities, deadlines, dependencies, accessibility and user control. |
| 552 | Email and Calendar Automation Safety | Govern drafting, recipients, scheduling, attachments, external sends, approvals and recall evidence. |
| 553 | Meeting Intelligence and Consent | Manage recording, transcription, summaries, attendance, consent, retention and correction. |
| 554 | Document Collaboration and Version Control | Govern collaborative documents, ownership, versions, comments, approvals and publication. |
| 555 | Enterprise Search and Knowledge Assistance | Provide access-filtered search, citations, freshness, privacy, explanation and user feedback. |
| 556 | Task Delegation and Approval Operations | Delegate work through scope, competence, deadlines, evidence, escalation and revocation. |
| 557 | Office Process Analytics without Surveillance | Measure process outcomes while prohibiting invasive individual tracking and ranking. |
| 558 | Knowledge Worker Accessibility and Inclusion | Support assistive technology, flexible interaction, cognitive accessibility and alternatives. |
| 559 | Workplace Change and Adoption | Plan communications, learning, support, feedback, resistance, benefits and fair transition. |
| 560 | Workplace Operations Assurance Gate | Gate workplace tools on consent, privacy, accessibility, delegation, fairness and support evidence. |
