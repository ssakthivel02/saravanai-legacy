# Releases 501–510 roadmap

    | Release | Capability | Intended outcome |
    |---:|---|---|
    | 501 | Decision Problem Registry | Register governed decision problems, owners, affected parties, constraints, deadlines and review state. |
| 502 | Evidence and Assumption Ledger | Record evidence, assumptions, uncertainty, provenance, expiry and challenge history for material decisions. |
| 503 | Scenario and Counterfactual Analysis | Compare plausible scenarios and counterfactuals using explicit assumptions, sensitivity and limitations. |
| 504 | Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis | Evaluate alternatives across weighted criteria, thresholds, trade-offs, conflicts and human judgement. |
| 505 | Decision Explanation and Traceability | Link decisions to evidence, alternatives, policies, approvers, conditions, outcomes and revisions. |
| 506 | Human Expert Review Workflow | Coordinate qualified expert review, conflicts, evidence requests, deadlines and final accountability. |
| 507 | Decision Outcome Monitoring | Monitor intended and unintended outcomes, drift, affected groups, corrective actions and review dates. |
| 508 | Bias Fairness and Distributional Review | Assess decision impacts across relevant groups using justified methods and human interpretation. |
| 509 | Decision Appeal and Reconsideration | Provide challenge, appeal, correction, independent reconsideration, deadlines and redress evidence. |
| 510 | Decision Intelligence Assurance Gate | Gate decision systems on evidence, assumptions, alternatives, fairness, explanation, appeal and outcomes. |
