- Media operations depend on rights, consent, provenance, accessibility and correction.
- Integration fabric depends on identity, schemas, egress, delivery and portability.
- Cyber recovery depends on criticality, immutable backups, identity and rebuild evidence.
- Identity trust depends on proofing, credentials, recovery, privacy and redress.
- Workplace operations depend on consent, privacy, accessibility and fair measurement.
- Public-safety patterns depend on verification, safeguarding and continuity.
- Enterprise economics depends on transparent assumptions, budgets and hard stops.
- Global experience depends on locale, scripts, accessibility, culture and legal review.
- Enterprise v5 completion depends on evidence from all preceding domains.
