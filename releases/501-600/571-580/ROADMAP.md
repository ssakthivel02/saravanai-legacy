# Releases 571–580 roadmap

    | Release | Capability | Intended outcome |
    |---:|---|---|
    | 571 | Cost Model and Unit Economics Registry | Register cost drivers, units, assumptions, owners, evidence, sensitivity and review dates. |
| 572 | Budget Planning and Scenario Analysis | Model budgets, scenarios, constraints, uncertainty, reserves, approvals and hard stops. |
| 573 | FinOps Allocation and Showback v2 | Allocate consumption transparently across tenants, products, services and shared platforms. |
| 574 | Investment Portfolio Prioritisation | Prioritise investments using value, risk, dependency, capacity, strategy and evidence. |
| 575 | Benefits Realisation and Outcome Verification | Verify baselines, benefits, disbenefits, attribution, evidence and accountable owners. |
| 576 | Vendor Cost and Contract Optimisation | Assess supplier pricing, commitments, utilisation, risk, exit, disputes and savings evidence. |
| 577 | Consumption Quota and Entitlement Planning | Plan quotas, eligibility, fairness, expiry, alerts and revocation without enabling billing. |
| 578 | Economic Risk and Sensitivity Testing | Stress-test demand, prices, capacity, supplier and operational assumptions. |
| 579 | Value Transparency and Executive Reporting | Report cost, outcomes, risks, assumptions, uncertainty and corrective actions. |
| 580 | Enterprise Economics Assurance Gate | Gate economic decisions on assumptions, budgets, allocation, value, supplier and stress evidence. |
