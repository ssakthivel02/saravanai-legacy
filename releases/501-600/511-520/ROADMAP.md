# Releases 511–520 roadmap

    | Release | Capability | Intended outcome |
    |---:|---|---|
    | 511 | Enterprise Digital Asset Registry | Register images, audio, video, documents, owners, rights, versions, provenance and lifecycle. |
| 512 | Image Generation and Editing Governance | Govern generated and edited images through source, consent, safety, disclosure and review controls. |
| 513 | Audio Generation and Voice Safety | Protect voice identity, consent, disclosure, cloning restrictions, review and revocation. |
| 514 | Video Generation and Editing Safety | Govern video synthesis, editing, likeness, sensitive contexts, provenance and publication approval. |
| 515 | Document and PDF Generation Pipeline | Generate governed documents through templates, source traceability, accessibility, validation and approval. |
| 516 | Brand Asset and Design System Governance | Control logos, typography, components, usage rules, accessibility, localisation and retirement. |
| 517 | Rights Licensing and Consent Management | Track licences, consent, territorial scope, duration, restrictions, attribution and withdrawal. |
| 518 | Synthetic Media Provenance and Disclosure v2 | Attach provenance, synthetic disclosure, tamper evidence, limitations and correction channels. |
| 519 | Media Accessibility and Localisation | Provide captions, transcripts, descriptions, translations, alternatives and native review. |
| 520 | Multimodal Media Assurance Gate | Gate media release on rights, consent, provenance, safety, accessibility and correction evidence. |
