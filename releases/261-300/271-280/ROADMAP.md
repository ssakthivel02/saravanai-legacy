# Releases 271–280 roadmap

    | Release | Capability | Intended outcome |
    |---:|---|---|
    | 271 | Enterprise Knowledge Publishing | Publish governed knowledge products with owners, provenance, versions, audiences and retirement. |
| 272 | Content Lifecycle and Editorial Governance | Manage content through commissioning, drafting, review, approval, correction, expiry and archival. |
| 273 | Multimodal Content Generation Safety | Govern generated text, image, audio and video through provenance, disclosure, consent and safety review. |
| 274 | Translation Transliteration and Terminology | Govern multilingual translation, transliteration, scripts, terminology, confidence and native review. |
| 275 | Search Discovery and Recommendation Governance | Operate ranking, search and recommendations with access filtering, explainability, freshness and user controls. |
| 276 | Learning Assessment and Credential Readiness | Deliver learning paths and assessments with accessibility, integrity and evidence without claiming accreditation. |
| 277 | Children and Family Experience Safety | Protect children through age-appropriate content, parental controls, safeguarding and restricted profiling. |
| 278 | Religious Cultural and Heritage Integrity | Separate documented history, traditional belief, scholarly interpretation and unverified claims respectfully. |
| 279 | Public Knowledge Transparency and Corrections | Publish source, confidence, update history, limitations, correction and redress information. |
| 280 | Knowledge and Experience Assurance Gate | Gate knowledge release on provenance, editorial, safety, language, accessibility, child and cultural evidence. |
