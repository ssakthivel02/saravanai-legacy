# Releases 281–290 roadmap

    | Release | Capability | Intended outcome |
    |---:|---|---|
    | 281 | Enterprise Control Library | Maintain controls, objectives, owners, evidence expectations, tests and lifecycle mappings. |
| 282 | Automated Evidence Collection | Collect evidence through authorised sources, minimisation, integrity hashes, expiry and review. |
| 283 | Audit Planning and Fieldwork | Plan audit scope, samples, procedures, findings, evidence, independence and closure. |
| 284 | Regulatory Obligation Management | Track obligations, jurisdictions, applicability, owners, deadlines, controls and legal review. |
| 285 | Privacy Operations and Rights Automation | Coordinate access, correction, deletion, portability and objection requests with identity verification and legal holds. |
| 286 | Model Risk Management | Classify AI models by impact, validate limitations, testing, approvals, monitoring and retirement. |
| 287 | Third-Party Assurance and Monitoring | Monitor suppliers, subprocessors, attestations, findings, service changes, exit and review dates. |
| 288 | Business Continuity and Crisis Exercises | Run continuity, cyber, supplier, workforce and communication exercises with objectives and corrective actions. |
| 289 | Executive Risk and Compliance Reporting | Present evidence-backed risk, control, audit, privacy, supplier and resilience indicators. |
| 290 | Trust and Compliance Assurance Gate | Gate trust readiness on control, evidence, audit, regulatory, privacy, model, supplier and continuity evidence. |
