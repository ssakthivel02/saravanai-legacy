# Roadmap

| Release | Capability | Outcome |
|---:|---|---|
| 111 | Industry Solution Architecture | Reusable industry patterns with assumptions, controls, boundaries and qualified review. |
| 112 | Health and Life Sciences Safety Pattern | Non-clinical health architecture with high-stakes review and prohibited diagnostic claims. |
| 113 | Financial Services Control Pattern | Financial information boundaries, fraud controls, four-eyes approval and evidence retention. |
| 114 | Public Sector and Critical Infrastructure Pattern | Resilience, supply-chain, privileged-access and public-safety boundaries. |
| 115 | Education and Child Safety Pattern | Age-appropriate design, safeguarding, parental controls and restricted profiling. |
| 116 | Media and Publishing Integrity Pattern | Editorial provenance, corrections, synthetic-media disclosure and accountability. |
| 117 | Religious and Cultural Heritage Integrity | Respectful classification of documented, traditional, scholarly and unverified claims. |
| 118 | Developer Platform and SDK Governance | Stable SDK versioning, permission boundaries, examples, deprecation and security review. |
| 119 | Template and Catalogue Marketplace Governance | Template ownership, provenance, licensing, safety review and revocation. |
| 120 | Industry Readiness Assurance Gate | Domain safety, legal review, evidence and operational readiness before enablement. |
