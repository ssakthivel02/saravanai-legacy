# Incident response

1. Detect and assign severity.
2. Preserve request IDs, audit events and deployment evidence.
3. Contain by disabling affected feature flags, keys, routes or tenants.
4. Eradicate the cause; rotate secrets where exposure is suspected.
5. Recover from a known-good release and validate integrity.
6. Notify affected parties according to applicable contractual and legal duties.
7. Complete a blameless review and track corrective actions.
