# Releases 201–210 roadmap

    | Release | Capability | Intended outcome |
    |---:|---|---|
    | 201 | Geopolitical and Regional Risk Intelligence | Track regional dependencies, sanctions-screening boundaries, service exposure, local review and continuity implications. |
| 202 | Crisis Information Integrity | Publish emergency and crisis information only with source verification, timestamps, corrections, authority and escalation. |
| 203 | Vulnerability and Patch Governance | Manage vulnerabilities, affected assets, exploitability, remediation SLAs, exceptions, patch evidence and compensating controls. |
| 204 | Software Supply-Chain Provenance | Require dependency inventory, SBOM, signatures, build provenance, vulnerability review and revocation capability. |
| 205 | Identity Threat Detection and Response | Detect suspicious authentication, impossible travel, session theft, privilege escalation and service-account misuse. |
| 206 | Privileged and Insider Risk Controls | Govern privileged activity through just-in-time access, four-eyes approval, session evidence, separation and revocation. |
| 207 | Fraud Account Abuse and Misuse Prevention | Detect account farming, credential abuse, automation misuse, quota evasion and fraudulent workflows with appeal paths. |
| 208 | Cyber Recovery and Resilience Operations | Coordinate immutable backup, restore, isolated recovery, dependency validation, communications and return-to-service evidence. |
| 209 | Transparency Reporting and Redress | Publish explainable trust metrics, incident summaries, policy changes, complaint handling and correction mechanisms. |
| 210 | Global Trust and Resilience Assurance Gate | Gate global operation on regional risk, crisis integrity, vulnerabilities, supply chain, identity, abuse and recovery evidence. |
