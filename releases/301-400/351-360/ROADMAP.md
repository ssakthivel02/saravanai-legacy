# Releases 351–360 roadmap

    | Release | Capability | Intended outcome |
    |---:|---|---|
    | 351 | Enterprise Customer Workspace v2 | Provide tenant-scoped projects, artefacts, approvals, support, usage and export controls. |
| 352 | Partner and Reseller Workspace | Provide partner onboarding, delegated roles, opportunities, support and evidence boundaries. |
| 353 | Marketplace Publisher Onboarding | Govern publisher identity, ownership, licensing, safety evidence, support and revocation. |
| 354 | Capability Subscription and Entitlement Readiness | Model capabilities, quotas, periods, eligibility and revocation without activating billing. |
| 355 | Customer Health and Adoption Intelligence | Assess adoption, outcomes, support, reliability and risks using privacy-safe indicators. |
| 356 | Service Review and Success Planning | Plan service reviews, outcomes, actions, owners, dates and evidence with customers. |
| 357 | Complaint Redress and Ombudsman Readiness | Manage complaints, evidence, escalation, redress, deadlines and independent review readiness. |
| 358 | Partner Performance and Risk Monitoring | Assess obligations, support, security, customer impact, findings and exit readiness. |
| 359 | Ecosystem Commercial Readiness without Billing | Assess contracts, entitlements, cost transparency and disputes while billing remains disabled. |
| 360 | Customer and Partner Assurance Gate | Gate ecosystem capability on customer, partner, support, privacy, redress and exit evidence. |
