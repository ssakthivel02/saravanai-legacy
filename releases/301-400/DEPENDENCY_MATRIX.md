# Dependency matrix

- Digital twins depend on topology, data contracts, evidence and simulation controls.
- Agent engineering depends on identity, AI safety, tool governance, approvals and observability.
- Cryptographic agility depends on secrets, certificates, inventory and incident response.
- Platform engineering depends on GitOps, supply-chain, policy and environment governance.
- Privacy-enhancing analytics depends on data contracts, legal review and qualified cryptography review.
- Customer and partner ecosystem depends on entitlements, support, privacy, redress and exit.
- Industry patterns depend on qualified domain review and explicit high-stakes boundaries.
- Global resilience depends on SRE, backup, suppliers, crisis communication and recovery.
- Knowledge trust depends on provenance, accessibility, child safety and cultural review.
- Enterprise v3 completion depends on evidence from all nine preceding domains.
