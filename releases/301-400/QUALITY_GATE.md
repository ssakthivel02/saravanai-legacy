# Programme quality gate

A merged design pack is not a production approval.

Production approval requires:
- Existing and programme workflows passing.
- Non-production schema and migration evidence.
- Threat, privacy, accessibility and resilience review.
- Cost and provider-dependency review.
- Rollback and restore evidence.
- Qualified review for high-stakes or culturally sensitive domains.
- Residual-risk ownership and expiry.
- Multi-party go/no-go approval.
