# Releases 361–370 roadmap

    | Release | Capability | Intended outcome |
    |---:|---|---|
    | 361 | Energy and Utilities OT Safety Pattern | Define segmentation, privileged access, maintenance, safety impact, recovery and human control for OT. |
| 362 | Healthcare Clinical Boundary Pattern | Enforce non-diagnostic boundaries, clinical review, patient safety, confidentiality and escalation. |
| 363 | Financial Services Conduct Pattern | Enforce non-advice boundaries, suitability, fraud, four-eyes review and records controls. |
| 364 | Public Sector Transparency Pattern | Govern public accountability, accessibility, records, procurement, redress and service continuity. |
| 365 | Education and Safeguarding Pattern | Protect learners through age-appropriate design, safeguarding, accessibility and restricted profiling. |
| 366 | Legal Services Confidentiality Pattern | Govern privilege, confidentiality, conflicts, legal holds, human review and records. |
| 367 | Media Provenance and Election Integrity Pattern | Govern sources, synthetic media, corrections, political sensitivity and publication accountability. |
| 368 | Religious and Cultural Heritage Pattern v2 | Separate documented, traditional, scholarly and unverified claims with respectful review. |
| 369 | Critical Infrastructure Cross-Sector Exercise | Exercise cascading dependencies, communications, recovery, suppliers and public safety. |
| 370 | Regulated Industry Assurance Gate | Gate industry patterns on qualified review, boundaries, evidence, continuity and redress. |
