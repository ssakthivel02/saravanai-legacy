# Releases 461–470 roadmap

    | Release | Capability | Intended outcome |
    |---:|---|---|
    | 461 | Sustainability Data and Methodology Registry | Register energy, carbon, water and waste methods, assumptions, owners and verification status. |
| 462 | Carbon-Aware Workload Scheduling v2 | Schedule flexible workloads using approved signals without weakening reliability or safety. |
| 463 | Energy Efficiency Engineering | Measure and improve compute, storage, network and model efficiency with evidence. |
| 464 | Hardware Lifecycle and Circularity | Govern procurement, repair, reuse, secure erasure, recycling and supplier evidence. |
| 465 | Environmental Risk and Dependency Assessment | Assess climate, energy, water, location and supplier exposure with recovery actions. |
| 466 | Worker Wellbeing and Responsible Automation | Assess automation impact, workload, training, appeal, fairness and prohibited surveillance. |
| 467 | Community and Public Interest Impact | Evaluate affected communities, accessibility, cultural impact, harms, benefits and redress. |
| 468 | Responsible Innovation Review Board | Review high-impact innovations through safety, privacy, ethics, inclusion and evidence. |
| 469 | Sustainability Claims and Disclosure | Govern public claims, methodology, boundaries, evidence, corrections and independent review. |
| 470 | Sustainability and Social Impact Gate | Gate impact claims and operations on methodology, safety, workforce and community evidence. |
