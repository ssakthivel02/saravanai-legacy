# Dependency matrix

- AI operations depend on model governance, safety, evaluations and cost controls.
- Sovereign and edge AI depend on device trust, signing, custody and offline recovery.
- Security operations depend on telemetry, identity, incident and evidence controls.
- Data platforms depend on contracts, lineage, privacy, retention and portability.
- Business integration depends on transaction controls, master data and reconciliation.
- Customer experience depends on consent, accessibility, support and redress.
- Sustainability depends on transparent methodology, safety and public-interest review.
- Engineering intelligence depends on SDLC, traceability, privacy and fair measurement.
- Global trust depends on qualified legal review, records and independent assurance.
- Enterprise v4 completion depends on evidence from all preceding domains.

No pack bypasses controls introduced by Releases 021–400.
