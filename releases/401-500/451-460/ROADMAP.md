# Releases 451–460 roadmap

    | Release | Capability | Intended outcome |
    |---:|---|---|
    | 451 | Omnichannel Journey Orchestration | Coordinate web, mobile, email, chat and support journeys with consent and continuity. |
| 452 | Customer Identity and Preference Centre | Manage identity links, communication preferences, consent, delegation and revocation. |
| 453 | Accessible Experience Personalisation | Personalise experiences using explicit controls, accessible alternatives and opt-out. |
| 454 | Customer Notification and Communication Safety | Govern templates, recipients, urgency, approvals, quiet hours, evidence and correction. |
| 455 | Conversational AI Customer Support | Operate bounded support assistants with knowledge grounding, handoff, privacy and transcript controls. |
| 456 | Case Complaint and Escalation Operations | Manage cases, deadlines, evidence, complaints, redress, escalation and independent review. |
| 457 | Customer Feedback and Research Operations | Collect consented feedback, research evidence, themes, bias review and decision linkage. |
| 458 | Customer Trust Transparency Centre | Publish data use, AI use, limitations, corrections, status, redress and accessibility information. |
| 459 | Customer Experience Reliability and SLO | Measure journey availability, latency, correctness, abandonment and recovery objectives. |
| 460 | Customer Experience Assurance Gate | Gate omnichannel experience on consent, accessibility, safety, support, redress and reliability evidence. |
