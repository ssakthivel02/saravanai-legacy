# Releases 481–490 roadmap

    | Release | Capability | Intended outcome |
    |---:|---|---|
    | 481 | Global Privacy Jurisdiction Registry | Register privacy jurisdictions, applicability, owners, legal review and effective dates. |
| 482 | Cross-Border Data Transfer Governance | Govern transfer purpose, regions, safeguards, suppliers, evidence and suspension. |
| 483 | Consent and Lawful Basis Operations v2 | Operate consent, withdrawal, lawful basis, purpose, evidence and downstream propagation. |
| 484 | Digital Identity Trust Framework | Define identity assurance, credentials, federation, recovery, fraud and redress requirements. |
| 485 | Electronic Signature and Evidence Readiness | Govern signing intent, identity, document integrity, timestamp, retention and dispute evidence. |
| 486 | Records Management and Defensible Disposal | Classify records, retention, holds, disposition, approvals and destruction evidence. |
| 487 | AI Regulatory Readiness Register | Track AI obligations, risk classification, technical documentation, testing and qualified review. |
| 488 | Digital Trust Transparency Reporting | Report security, privacy, AI, accessibility, incidents, corrections and redress with evidence. |
| 489 | Independent Audit and Attestation Readiness | Prepare scoped evidence, findings, remediation and management assertions without claiming certification. |
| 490 | Global Digital Trust Assurance Gate | Gate global trust on privacy, identity, signatures, records, AI and independent review evidence. |
