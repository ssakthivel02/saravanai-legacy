# Releases 401–410 roadmap

    | Release | Capability | Intended outcome |
    |---:|---|---|
    | 401 | Enterprise AI Capability Registry | Register AI capabilities, owners, versions, approved uses, limitations and retirement state. |
| 402 | Model Routing and Selection Policy | Route requests across approved models using safety, privacy, quality, latency and cost constraints. |
| 403 | Prompt Programme and Template Governance | Version prompts, variables, owners, test cases, approvals, redaction and rollback. |
| 404 | Retrieval Pipeline Orchestration | Govern retrieval sources, access filters, chunking, ranking, freshness, citations and fallback. |
| 405 | AI Evaluation Campaign Management | Plan benchmark suites, datasets, evaluators, thresholds, evidence and release decisions. |
| 406 | Inference Capacity and Queue Management | Manage concurrency, quotas, prioritisation, timeouts, degraded modes and hard budget stops. |
| 407 | AI Feature Flag and Rollout Control | Release AI capabilities through disabled defaults, tenant pilots, kill switches and rollback. |
| 408 | AI Quality Incident Management | Coordinate hallucination, safety, citation, privacy and service-quality incidents with evidence. |
| 409 | AI Portfolio Value and Risk Reporting | Report capability outcomes, adoption, cost, residual risk and remediation with accountable owners. |
| 410 | Enterprise AI Operating System Assurance Gate | Gate AI operations on registry, routing, prompts, retrieval, evaluation, capacity and incident evidence. |
