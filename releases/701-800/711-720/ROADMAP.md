# Releases 711–720 roadmap

    | Release | Capability | Intended outcome |
    |---:|---|---|
    | 711 | Research Question and Scope Registry | Register research questions, scope, audience, deadline, exclusions, owner and decision context. |
| 712 | Source Discovery and Authority Ranking | Discover sources and rank authority, relevance, recency, independence, jurisdiction and limitations. |
| 713 | Primary Source Acquisition and Preservation | Acquire authorised source copies, metadata, hashes, access conditions and preservation evidence. |
| 714 | Claim Evidence and Citation Graph | Link claims to exact source locations, versions, confidence, contradictions and review status. |
| 715 | Research Synthesis and Argument Mapping | Synthesize evidence, viewpoints, assumptions, uncertainty, gaps and counterarguments. |
| 716 | Fact Verification and Temporal Validation | Verify current-state claims, dates, office-holders, versions and other time-sensitive facts. |
| 717 | Research Quality and Bias Review | Review source diversity, conflicts, selection bias, framing, unsupported inference and limitations. |
| 718 | Research Report and Briefing Composer | Compose accessible reports, briefs, tables and appendices from verified evidence and templates. |
| 719 | Research Correction and Retraction Workflow | Correct or retract claims, update citations, notify consumers and preserve revision history. |
| 720 | Deep Research Assurance Gate | Gate research outputs on scope, sources, citations, verification, balance, accessibility and correction. |
