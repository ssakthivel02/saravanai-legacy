# Releases 781–790 roadmap

    | Release | Capability | Intended outcome |
    |---:|---|---|
    | 781 | Enterprise Strategy and Objective Registry | Register strategic objectives, measures, owners, dependencies, assumptions and review cycles. |
| 782 | Portfolio Investment and Capacity Planning | Allocate investment and capacity using value, risk, dependencies, constraints and scenarios. |
| 783 | Programme Milestone and Dependency Control | Track milestones, dependencies, critical paths, evidence, forecasts, escalation and decisions. |
| 784 | Enterprise Risk and Opportunity Portfolio | Aggregate risks and opportunities with ownership, exposure, treatment, evidence and review. |
| 785 | Executive Decision Brief and Board Pack | Compose concise evidence-backed decision briefs, options, assumptions, risks and conditions. |
| 786 | Operational Resilience Command View | Present critical services, incidents, dependencies, recovery, capacity and executive actions. |
| 787 | Crisis Command and Stakeholder Coordination | Coordinate roles, facts, decisions, actions, communications, welfare and transition. |
| 788 | Benefits Outcomes and Value Realisation | Verify outcomes, attribution, disbenefits, customer impact, evidence and corrective actions. |
| 789 | Enterprise Performance and Trust Reporting | Report value, reliability, safety, privacy, accessibility, cost and residual risk transparently. |
| 790 | Executive Intelligence and Resilience Gate | Gate executive reporting on evidence, uncertainty, dependencies, risks, outcomes and accountability. |
