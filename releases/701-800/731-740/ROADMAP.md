# Releases 731–740 roadmap

    | Release | Capability | Intended outcome |
    |---:|---|---|
    | 731 | Enterprise Communication Campaign Registry | Register communication purpose, audience, channels, owners, approvals, timing and success measures. |
| 732 | Email Drafting and Recipient Safety | Draft email with grounded context, recipient validation, attachment checks, approval and recall evidence. |
| 733 | Calendar Scheduling and Meeting Coordination | Coordinate availability, time zones, attendees, rooms, accessibility, reminders and changes. |
| 734 | Omnichannel Customer Contact Profile | Unify consented contact preferences, identity, language, accessibility and channel history. |
| 735 | Customer Request and Case Intake | Capture requests, identity, category, urgency, evidence, consent, ownership and service deadlines. |
| 736 | Case Resolution and Knowledge Guidance | Guide agents using approved knowledge, citations, next actions, escalation and human judgement. |
| 737 | Complaint Appeal and Redress Operations | Manage complaints, independent review, evidence, deadlines, remedies and outcome communication. |
| 738 | Service Quality and Conversation Review | Review accuracy, empathy, accessibility, privacy, compliance, resolution and customer effort. |
| 739 | Customer Communication Correction and Withdrawal | Correct inaccurate messages, stop campaigns, notify affected recipients and preserve evidence. |
| 740 | Communications and Customer Operations Gate | Gate communication services on recipients, consent, accuracy, accessibility, redress and quality. |
