# Dependency matrix

- AI evaluation depends on protected datasets, reproducible baselines and remediation.
- Research depends on authoritative sources, citations, temporal checks and corrections.
- Application creation depends on secure design, accessibility, tests and deployment controls.
- Communications depend on recipient safety, consent, accuracy, complaints and redress.
- Learning depends on source integrity, fairness, privacy and no fabricated achievements.
- OT and IoT depend on operational safety, identity, maintenance and manual fallback.
- Hybrid infrastructure depends on landing zones, IaC, networking, backup and migration.
- Data products depend on contracts, semantics, quality, lineage and consumer transition.
- Executive intelligence depends on evidence, uncertainty, risks, outcomes and accountability.
- Enterprise v7 completion depends on evidence from all preceding domains.

No pack bypasses controls introduced by Releases 021–700.
