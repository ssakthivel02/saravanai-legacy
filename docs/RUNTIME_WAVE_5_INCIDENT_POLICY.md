# Runtime Wave 5 Incident Policy

SEV1: security risk, data-integrity risk, full outage or at least 50% user impact.
SEV2: at least 20% impact or at least two hours duration.
SEV3: at least 5% impact or at least 30 minutes duration.
SEV4: limited impact below those thresholds.

Classification is advisory. The runtime does not page responders, send messages or create an incident record.
